A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Make room for what’s next.

A disk is one long strip of space. Now shows it as it is; After shows your plan. Select a region to rename, resize, format, split or delete it, or drag the boundary between two regions. Undo steps back one change at a time.

Nothing is written while you plan. Review lists every change and names any partition whose data would be erased. Only Apply plan, with administrator approval, changes the disk.

Boundary handles also work from the keyboard: arrow keys move by 1 MiB, with Shift by 1 GiB, and Home / End jump to the limits.
